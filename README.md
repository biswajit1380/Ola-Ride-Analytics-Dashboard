# Ola Ride Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Ola ride data and generate actionable business insights. The dashboard focuses on ride bookings, revenue trends, cancellations, vehicle performance, and customer & driver ratings to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyze ride booking patterns, identify revenue drivers, understand cancellation behavior, and evaluate performance across different vehicle types using Power BI.

---

## 📊 Key Features

* Interactive and dynamic Power BI dashboard
* Date slicers for flexible time-based analysis
* KPIs for total bookings, revenue, success rate, and cancellations
* Drill-down analysis by vehicle type and payment method

---

## 🔍 Business Insights

* Sedan and Prime category rides show a higher success rate compared to other vehicle types
* Customer cancellations peak during evening hours
* Cash and UPI contribute the highest share of total revenue
* Auto rides have lower average distance but consistent booking volume
* Driver and customer ratings remain stable across vehicle categories
